Standard Clean

Recurring light maintenance — ideal weekly, biweekly, or monthly.

  • Dust surfaces, furniture & décor
  • Vacuum & mop floors throughout
  • Kitchen counters, sink & appliance exteriors
  • Bathrooms — toilets, showers, sinks & mirrors
  • Beds made, trash emptied
  • Mirrors & glass, streak-free

Goal: keep a home consistently fresh, visit after visit.

Deep Clean

A comprehensive reset — ideal for first-time visits or seasonal refreshes.

  • Everything in Standard, plus:
  • Baseboards, door frames & switches detailed
  • Blinds, vents & ceiling fans dusted
  • Interior windows & sills cleaned
  • Cabinet fronts fully wiped
  • Tile, grout & showerheads scrubbed

Goal: reach the dust and grime a quick clean can't.

Move-In/Out & Post Construction

A full interior reset for empty homes and fresh builds.

  • Everything in Deep Clean, plus:
  • Inside oven, fridge & freezer
  • Inside cabinets, drawers & closets
  • Interior windows & baseboards, spot to spot
  • Post-construction dust & paint spot-removal
  • Sticker & label removal

Goal: every space ready for its next chapter.

Commercial Standard

Serviced on your schedule — after hours, weekends, or between shifts.

  • Desks & high-touch surfaces disinfected
  • Restrooms fully cleaned & sanitized
  • Break room counters & appliances wiped
  • Trash removed, liners replaced
  • Common areas & entry glass, streak-free
  • Floors vacuumed & mopped

Goal: a consistently professional space, every visit.

Commercial Deep Clean

A full reset — ideal for first visits or before a big walkthrough.

  • Everything in Commercial Standard, plus:
  • High & low dusting — vents, ledges & fixtures
  • Blinds, baseboards & door frames detailed
  • Tile & grout scrubbed, fixtures descaled
  • Interior windows, sills & tracks cleaned
  • Break-room appliance interiors degreased

Goal: a true reset between tenants, or before opening.
