1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work, remember your preferences, understand how the site is used, and support advertising. We also use similar technologies such as pixels and local storage.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

When required, we ask for your consent through a cookie banner the first time you visit. You can accept all, reject non-essential cookies, or manage categories. You can change your choice at any time using the button below.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how the site works. To opt out of certain advertising, visit aboutads.info/choices or your device's ad settings.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third parties (for example Meta and Google) to provide analytics and advertising. These parties process data under their own privacy policies.

5. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy as our practices or the law change. The effective date above reflects the latest version.
