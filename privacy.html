Política de Privacidad

Fecha de vigencia: 4 de agosto de 2026 · Última actualización: 4 de agosto de 2026

Dream Clean by Dalila Cerezo LLC, doing business as Dream Clean by DC ("Dream Clean," "we," "us," or "our"), respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your information when you visit our websites — including dreamcleanbydc.com and our related subdomains (card, atrium, portal) — request an estimate, or use our cleaning services.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide to us

  • Contact and booking details: full name, email address, phone number, and service address submitted through our instant-estimate form or when you book a service.
  • Service details: type of cleaning, approximate square footage, number of bedrooms/bathrooms, frequency, add-ons, pets in the home, and any notes you share.
  • Communications: messages, reviews, referral information, and discount codes you provide.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage and device data: IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, and referring pages, collected through cookies and similar technologies.
  • Cookies and pixels: including essential, analytics, and advertising cookies (such as the Meta Pixel and Google Analytics). See our Cookie Policy for details and choices.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To prepare estimates, schedule, and deliver cleaning services.
  • To communicate with you about appointments, service updates, follow-ups, and support.
  • To send marketing communications (calls, texts, emails) where you have consented — you may opt out at any time.
  • To process referrals, discounts, and loyalty offers.
  • To operate, secure, and improve our websites and services.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms & Conditions.

3. SMS / Text Messaging

If you opt in, we may send you text messages such as appointment reminders, service updates, and marketing messages using automated technology. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchase. Mobile opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Service providers who help us operate — for example website hosting (Vercel), scheduling, payment processing, email/SMS delivery, and analytics.
  • Advertising partners such as Meta and Google, through cookies/pixels, to measure and improve advertising (subject to your cookie choices).
  • Legal and safety recipients when required by law, to protect our rights, or in connection with a business transfer.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to run our site, remember preferences, analyze traffic, and support advertising. When required, we ask for your consent through our cookie banner. You can manage or withdraw consent at any time. Learn more in our Cookie Policy.

6. Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes described here, to provide services, and to meet legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. When no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

7. Your Choices & Rights

  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.
  • Opt out of marketing emails (unsubscribe link) and texts (reply STOP).
  • Manage cookies through our banner and your browser settings.

Depending on your state, you may have additional rights (for example, under the Florida Digital Bill of Rights). To exercise any right, contact us using the details below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services and websites are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Third-Party Links

Our sites may link to third-party websites (for example Instagram). We are not responsible for their privacy practices; please review their policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date reflects the most recent changes. Material changes will be posted on this page.
